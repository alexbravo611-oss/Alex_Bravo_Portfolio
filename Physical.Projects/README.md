## Project Overview
Our final project was an automated lightbulb testing and sorting system.  
A robotic arm first picked up each lightbulb and placed it into a custom‑designed testing fixture connected to a breadboard circuit. The circuit used **green and red LEDs** to determine whether the bulb was functional or defective, storing the result in a “good” or “bad” variable using binary numbers.

After testing, the robotic arm placed the bulb onto a conveyor belt equipped with two sensors, one at the start and one at the end. When the first sensor detected the bulb, the conveyor activated and ran until the second sensor confirmed arrival. Once both sensors were occupied, the robotic arm moved to the end of the conveyor and sorted each bulb into its designated location based on its test result.
